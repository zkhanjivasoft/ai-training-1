Proposed change:
Claude found that the error message in ActivityFeed was missing the `role="alert"`
attribute that every other error surface in the app uses. This was flagged as known
drift in the project's own documented frontend conventions (.claude/rules/frontend.md,
rule FE-034), which explicitly calls out this exact line as "drift to fix, not a
pattern to copy." Adding role="alert" makes the error announced immediately by screen
readers, consistent with the rest of the app.
